Book : Sapiens

Author: Yuval Noah Harari

Summary: Sapiens is a book that explores the history of humanity from the perspective of the species Homo sapiens. It covers the cognitive, agricultural, and scientific revolutions that have shaped human society.

Learnings: I learned about the power of shared beliefs and how they have enabled humans to cooperate in large numbers. The book also highlighted the importance of understanding our past to better navigate the future.

Perspective: It filled me with great positivity towards the future specially the AI, which can make our world a better place.
